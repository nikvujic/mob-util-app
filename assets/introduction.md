# Welcome to The App

Notes, a shopping list, a day planner and a few handy tools in one place, made to be used with **one hand**. Everything stays on your phone.

## Getting around
- The bar at the bottom switches between **Notes**, **Shop**, **Planner** and **Hub**.
- The **menu** (☰, or a swipe right along the bottom bar) has Security, Themes, Backup, Notifications and Settings. Pip, the little robot at its top, is the app's mascot; he lives in the field at the bottom of the menu. Until he's hatched, his egg sits there: tap it until it opens. His name and age show above him. Once he's out, he has his own page in the Hub, **Pip**: swipe his card for his settings (rename him, play his animations, pick his background). The battery beside his name is his energy: he gets tired as it runs down, and turns off when it's empty. A battery comes every few hours when you open the app (up to 3); tap his room to give him one. He sleeps from 23:00 to 8:00.
- Buttons sit at the bottom, on the side of the hand you last touched with.
- **Reach mode**: at the top of a list, pull down again and the top comes into thumb reach; push up and it springs back.
- **Hold** an item to select it, then tap others to add them; the buttons at the bottom act on all of them.
- Hold a **drag handle** to move an item up or down.

## Notes
- **To-do**, under this note, is a to-do list: **+** adds things, **tap** one when it's done (it moves to Completed; tap it again to bring it back), **hold** one to change it, **Delete all** beside Completed clears the done ones. Settings turns it off.
- **+** starts a note; it's saved as you type.
- **Markdown**: start a line with a hash and a space for a heading (two, three or four hashes for smaller ones); wrap words in two stars for **bold**, one star for *italic*, two tildes to strike them out. The symbols hide when you're not on that line.
- **Lists**: start a line with a dash and a space, or a number and a dot. Enter continues the list; Enter on an empty item ends it.
- **Checkboxes**: a dash, a space and square brackets with a space inside make one; tap the box to tick it.
- **More formats**: backticks for `code`, three backticks on the lines above and below for a code block, a greater-than sign and a space for a quote, two equals signs on each side to ==highlight==, and three dashes alone on a line for a divider.
- **Format** (shown while typing) opens a row of buttons above the keyboard for all of these: they format what's selected (or the line), and pressing one again takes it off.
- **Undo** and **redo** step through your changes; **Discard** puts the note back as it was.
- **Lock** a note to encrypt it with your master password.
- Line numbers can be turned off in Settings.

## Shop
- **To buy** above, **Items** (things bought before) below. Tap an item to move it between the two.
- **+** adds to the end of To buy; the box stays open for the next one.
- The small button by an item to buy sets a **count** or a **weight**; tap the amount to change it.

## Planner
- The strip at the bottom picks the day.
- Each day is a **timeline**: tap free time to add a block that starts about where you tapped.
- Tap the **left edge**, by the hours, to add a **reminder** at that time.
- **Routines** repeat on the weekdays you pick; edit them from the Routines button.
- Blocks can have a **colour**, a **description** and a **reminder** before they start.
- Times that run into another block turn red; **Find a free time** moves them.
- Tapping a reminder's **notification** opens the planner at it.

## Hub
Extra tools, as tiles. Hold a tile and drag it to rearrange them.
- **Counters**: count anything with − and +, with a step, limits and a colour.
- **Placard**: a big line of text, sideways. Hold until it pops to count one up. Tap the top-right corner five times for its settings.
- **Medicine**: tap a dose when you take it. Each medicine has a history and a calendar. **Reminders** come at dose times (and again later, if a medicine is set to remind again). Mark a medicine **important** and taking it needs the button **held for 5 seconds**, so it isn't ticked by mistake (it fills up, then pops); a **red dot** on the tile and the Hub tab shows while an important medicine's dose is due. Doses due at the same time come as one notification; tapping it opens Medicine. The bell at the top opens its notification settings.

## Security
- A **master password** encrypts locked notes and can lock whole sections. Without it, locked data can't be read, so keep it safe.
- **Section locks** hide a whole section while the app is locked; under the Hub, each of its pages can be locked on its own.
- **Fingerprint** unlock can stand in for typing it.
- The app locks again a little after you leave it.
- Forgot the password? **Forgot master password** resets it by deleting what it protects. To make sure it's you, type RESET and then **hold** Reset for 10 seconds; a plain tap turns it off for a day (or until you next unlock).
- Locked notes can't be deleted, and a backup can't be restored over them, without the password.

## Backup
- **Export** saves everything to one file; **Import** restores it. Locked notes stay encrypted inside.
- **Restore part of a backup** brings back only what you pick (say, one note), added to what's there.
- **Automatic backup** (on Backup): pick a folder once (say, one Google Drive syncs) and the app writes a backup there once a day when you open it, keeping the last 7.
- Backup shows when you last exported. A week later, a **red dot** on the menu and an evening notification remind you to make a new one (turn it off on Backup).

## Notifications
- The menu's **Notifications** shows the latest ones (tap **All** for today's and yesterday's), even after they're gone from the phone; tap one to go to it, **Clear** empties the list.
- Under it, each page's notifications: on or off, **pause** for a while, **private** (no names on the lock screen), a **sound** (the phone's, one of the app's, or silent) and a **colour**.

## Settings
- Line numbers, the counter click and vibration, whether this introduction comes back after updates (and restoring it if deleted), and **Clear data**.
- If the phone blocks the app's notifications, Settings says so at the top.

# Welcome to The App

Notes, a shopping list, a day planner and a few handy tools in one place, made to be used with **one hand**. Everything stays on your phone.

## Getting around
- The bar at the bottom switches between **Notes**, **Shop**, **Planner** and **Hub**.
- The **menu** (☰, or a swipe right along the bottom bar) has Security, Themes, Backup, Notifications and Settings. At its bottom lives **Pip** (see below).
- Buttons sit at the bottom, on the side of the hand you last touched with.
- **Reach mode**: at the top of a list, pull down again and the top comes into thumb reach; push up and it springs back.
- **Hold** an item to select it, then tap others to add them; the buttons at the bottom act on all of them.
- Hold a **drag handle** to move an item up or down.

## Notes
- **To-do**, under this note, is a to-do list: **+** adds things, **tap** one when it's done (it moves to Completed; tap it again to bring it back where it was, or to the end if the list changed meanwhile), **hold** one to change it, **Delete all** beside Completed clears the done ones. The button beside **+** has its options: a **Waiting** list (drag things you're waiting on into it and back; off, they show with the rest, marked with an hourglass) and a **Lock** (with a master password, it opens only while the app is unlocked). Settings hides it (its list and options stay).
- **+** starts a note; it's saved as you type.
- **Markdown**: start a line with a hash and a space for a heading (two, three or four hashes for smaller ones); wrap words in two stars for **bold**, one star for *italic*, two tildes to strike them out. The symbols hide when you're not on that line.
- **Lists**: start a line with a dash and a space, or a number and a dot. Enter continues the list; Enter on an empty item ends it.
- **Checkboxes**: a dash, a space and square brackets with a space inside make one; tap the box to tick it.
- **More formats**: backticks for `code`, three backticks on the lines above and below for a code block (they show, faint, while the cursor is in it), a greater-than sign and a space for a quote, two equals signs on each side to ==highlight==, and three dashes alone on a line for a divider.
- **Format** (shown while typing) opens a row of buttons above the keyboard for these (all but strikethrough, which is typed): they format what's selected (or the line), and pressing one again takes it off.
- **Undo** and **redo** step through your changes; **Discard** puts the note back as it was.
- **Lock** a note to encrypt it with your master password.
- Line numbers can be turned off in Settings.

## Shop
- **To buy** above, **Items** (things bought before) below. Tap an item to move it between the two.
- **+** adds to the end of To buy; the box stays open for the next one. As you type, it offers your bought **Items** that match: tap one to put it back. Adding one that's there already moves it instead of making a copy.
- **Hold** items to select them; with one selected, **Rename** sits beside Delete.
- The small button by an item to buy sets a **count** or a **weight**; tap the amount to change it, or to **type** one ("1-2").
- The **magnifier** beside **+** searches the bought items: a field above the keyboard shows only those with what you type (the list to buy stays as it is; the field stays when the keyboard closes; × or Back closes it). Nothing found: **Add** what you typed.
- **A–Z** on the Items header shows the bought list alphabetically (again for its own order).

## Planner
- The strip at the bottom picks the day.
- Each day is a **timeline**: tap free time to add a block that starts about where you tapped.
- Tap the **left edge**, by the hours, to add a **reminder** at that time (or use the bell button at the bottom, which also **lists all your reminders**, the next first: tick several to delete them at once).
- **Routines** repeat on the weekdays you pick; edit them from the Routines button. They're just there: nothing to tick off, and they don't show on past days.
- Tick a block off when it's done. Ticked-off blocks on past days are **cleared by themselves**.
- Blocks can have a **colour**, a **description** and a **reminder** (new ones remind at their start; pick Off for none, or a time before).
- **Tap a block** to see it, edit it, delete it or **move it to another day** (tomorrow first, handy for a missed one): if its time is taken there, it tells you, and to what nearest free time it'll move.
- Times that run into another block turn red; **Find a free time** moves them.
- Tapping a reminder's **notification** opens the planner at it.
- While a block or routine is **on**, a quiet notification stays in the phone's tray, counting down to its end; a block's **Done** ticks it off (it goes once it ends or is ticked). Turn it off on the Notifications page.

## Hub
Extra tools, as tiles. Hold a tile and drag it to rearrange them.
- **Counters**: count anything with − and +, with a step, limits and a colour.
- **Placard**: a big line of text, sideways. Hold until it pops to count one up. Tap the top-right corner five times for its settings.
- **Pip**: his own page, once he's hatched (see below).
- **Food**: what you ate and how it agreed with you. **+** logs a meal (its kind guessed from the time): tap the food field for an index of your **foods**, your **recipes** (tap one to pick which of its ingredients to add) and the recipes' **ingredients**, narrowed as you type (or add a new name); putting the keyboard away, or Back, closes it; a food added twice flashes; tap one to take it out. A meal's day and time can be changed. Later, open the meal and **rate** it (five faces, bad to good; tap again to clear), with symptoms and a note; **Delete meal** is at the end. The month calendar on top shows each day in its worst meal's colour; tap a day to see it. **Foods** (beside +) renames foods (to another food's name, it merges the two) and gives them tags (dairy, gluten, spicy...; ingredients you've added are foods too, tagged here); **Statistics** shows which foods and kinds tend to go badly and which seem fine, once there are enough rated meals to tell (5 with it, and 5 without); tap one for its page: how its meals went against the rest, the symptoms after it, what it was eaten with, and every meal with it. **By symptom** shows, for each symptom you've logged, the foods it follows clearly more often than without them.
- **Recipes**: a recipe opens to read while cooking, and **keeps the screen on**. It shows its prep time, type, ingredients with their quantities, and how it's made (written like a note). **More** (at the bottom) puts its ingredients on the Shop list (**To Shop**: one already there with the same quantity just goes back on the list), duplicates it, copies it as text, or deletes it. **Edit** changes everything: **tap an ingredient** for its quantity (a count, a weight, teaspoons or tablespoons, picked like Shop's, or typed) or to delete it; **hold** ingredients to select several and remove them; drag the handle to move one; **Add** picks from the **ingredients list**. That list (the button beside +) is in sections: the first, *Ingredients*, stays on top, new sections go under it, and the others move when you drag them by the **right edge** of their name (where an ingredient's handle is; a line shows where it'll go); tap a section's name, or hold an ingredient, to rename or delete it. An ingredient can't be added twice: it says where it already is. **Import / export** (at the top) has three choices. **Export** copies the recipes you tick as text, for someone to import into their app. **Agent instructions** copies the rules for an AI agent (ChatGPT, Claude...): the recipe format, your ingredients and a detailed example. Paste them into the agent with what you'd like and it writes recipes to import. **Import** reads copied text and lists the recipes in it, with how many ingredients each has and which are new to you. One with the same name as a recipe you have starts unticked. Tick the ones you want and they're added as new recipes, and their new ingredients go into their sections.
- Pages saved as you type (a subscription, a date, a meal) ask **Save or Discard** when you go back after changing something, like a note.
- **Subscriptions**: what you pay for, as cards two to a row in your order (**hold** one and drag it to move it), coloured by their state: **active** (green, renewing on its day), **to cancel** (orange: still renewing, but you mean to cancel it before then), **cancelled** (blue: still on until a day, then *Ended*) and **inactive** (grey: lapsed, the card ran out, a payment failed). Each card has its price (a **~** when it's about that), its day, the **card** it's paid with and when that card **expires** (a month and year, as on the card; *Card unknown* if you didn't say; the expiry **orange** a week or less ahead and **red** once it's gone by; a warning if it expires before the next renewal), its reminder and a note; at the top, what the running ones come to a **month**. Tap one to change anything. A notification comes on its day at 09:00 (renews, cancel it, ends), and a reminder before if you set one (to cancel ones get 2 days before); one to cancel soon puts a **red dot** on the tile. Its notifications are on the Notifications page.
- **Packing**: packing lists ("Ski trip", "Beach"...), each a card showing how much is packed. Open one and its things are under its **sections** (Documents, Clothes...), each with a box: **tap anywhere on a row** to tick it (it stays where it is, struck through), and **Reset** unticks everything when you start packing again (it asks first). **Edit** changes it: the name; **+ Add** under a section adds to it (several in a row; a name can't be in a section twice); **tap a thing** to rename it or set **how many**; **hold** things to select and delete them; drag one by its handle, into another section too. Each list has **its own sections**: tap a section's name to rename or delete it (its items go with it, asked first), drag it by its **right edge** to move it, and **Add section** is the button by Done. **Back** asks to Save or Discard. **+** makes a new list, starting with the **default sections** (the button beside +, yours to change); Done brings you back to your lists. **More** → **Copy list** makes a new list from one: a name, and which sections come along (Documents and Clothes, say, without the ski gear), all unticked.
- **Converter**: unit pairs (temperature, length, weight, volume, speed, cooking), two boxes each, each named ("Pounds (lb)"). Type in either box and the other shows the value converted. Nothing is kept when you leave.
- **People**: the people you know, as boxes on a board (the top half of the page) that you **pinch to zoom** and **drag to move around**. Pinch, or pull the **zoom handle** in the corner down (out) or up (in); tap it for 100%. Or double tap and, on the second tap, keep your finger down and move it: down zooms in, up zooms out. Each box shows a pixel face for their age (baby to 80+, blue or pink), their name and age. **Tap** someone to centre on them and see their info below: birthday and age, a note, and their relations (tap one to go to that person). **Edit** changes their name, gender, birthday, whether they're **highlighted** (they stand out) or **deceased** (with the date, so their age stops there), and their **relations**: **Add relation** picks someone, then what they are (parent, child, partner, former partner, brother or sister, or other, with a word like "Kum"); what follows is added too: a parent's child's brothers and sisters become their children, and a new brother or sister gets the same parents (where that makes sense). Families draw themselves as a tree: partners side by side, one line down from a couple to their children. **Drag** a person to move just them. Set someone as the **main person** in Edit: the board opens on them, and the **options** (⋮) can center on them, **highlight their family** or **Tidy** (lays everyone out as family trees; it asks first); **Find** searches by name; **Add** makes a new person in the middle of the board. A birthday you set is added to **Dates** too (with a People mark: its reminders and note can be changed there, the rest only here), and **From Dates** copies one already there (the original stays). **Show in Dates** under a birthday chooses whether it's shown in Dates, with reminders. Back while editing asks whether to keep the changes. The **note** is at the end of their info, written on its own page with formatting, like a recipe's method; **Settings → Hide People notes** hides notes (showing them again asks for the password).
- **Spotify**: music in the morning. Turn it on, pick a **time**, the **days** and a **volume**: at that time the phone sets its music volume and presses play, so Spotify carries on with what it last played (it works with the app closed, after a restart, and even when Spotify itself was closed).
- **Dates**: birthdays, anniversaries, holidays and events on a month calendar (a day's circle in its dates' colours: split when they differ, with a border for two of a kind), with what's on **today** at the top, what's coming under it, and one-off dates gone by under **Past** (so they aren't lost): each card has the day, how soon, the age or which anniversary, and its kind, length and birth year, and its reminders by a bell; tap a day for its dates. **+** adds one (on the day you tapped): a title and its type, each with its own form: a **birthday** or **anniversary** comes every year (the year, if you know it, gives the age or which anniversary); a **holiday** (plain, **Skiing**, **Beach** or a **Trip**) or an **event** (plain or a **Wedding**, which reminds a week and a month before) is on a date and can **last** several days; *Other* can be either. Then a note, and up to **3 reminders** (how many days before, and when; a new one reminds a day and a week before, at 09:00). On someone's birthday a **green dot** shows on the Dates tile until you open it. Its notifications are on the Notifications page.
- **Medicine**: tap a dose when you take it. A medicine can be taken every day or on some days only (pick the weekdays). Each medicine has a history and a calendar. **Reminders** come at dose times (and again later, if a medicine is set to remind again). Mark a medicine **important** and taking it needs the button **held for 5 seconds**, so it isn't ticked by mistake (it fills up, then pops); a **red dot** on the tile and the Hub tab shows while an important medicine's dose is due. Doses due at the same time come as one notification; tapping it opens Medicine. Its notification settings are on the Notifications page (in the menu).

## Pip
- Pip, the little robot, is the app's mascot. He lives in the field at the bottom of the menu. Until he's hatched, his egg sits there: tap it until it opens. He comes out with two segments of energy and a battery for you to give him.
- His sky follows the time of day and the seasons (sunrise and sunset move through the year), with the moon in its real phase. His field has big **trees** (oaks or birches; now and then, and mostly in winter, pines) in their season's colours (blossom in spring, autumn's leaves falling, bare in winter), swaying, in new spots each time you open the app, and dresses up for **Halloween** (lanterns, bats), **New Year's Eve** (a pine with blinking lights, fireworks after dark), **Christmas** on the 7th of January (a badnjak in straw, a lit candle) and **Easter** (painted eggs). With the **weather** on (in Internet Access), his sky shows it: clouds, rain, snow (on the ground too), fog, and storms with lightning; in rain, snow or winter there are no flowers or butterflies, and under clouds no shooting stars. And **he lives it**: in a **thunderstorm** he waits it out in his **tent** (a battery you give him flies in), in **rain** he opens his **umbrella** and jumps in puddles, in **drizzle** he holds a hand out, in **snow** he builds a **snowman**, catches flakes on his tongue and throws a **snowball at you**, and in **fog** he walks slowly and gets lost now and then. Out on his adventure: a tent in a storm, snowmen among the bugs, slower in fog. Pip Home's Animations has them all.
- His **name and age** show above him; on the right, his **energy** (a battery: green, orange, red) and the batteries you hold.
- He gets tired as his energy runs down (a segment every 4 hours awake), and turns off when it's empty. A **battery** comes every 5 hours, even while the app is closed (up to 3); **tap his room** to give him one: he walks up, pulls his old battery out of his side, throws it away, and puts the new one in.
- He **sleeps** from 23:00 to 8:00; look in on him around then and you may see him fall asleep or wake up (with a yawn and a stretch). **Tap him three times** while he sleeps to wake him: at night for 15 minutes, once a night (to give him a battery or let him charge; he's very tired), from 07:00 for the day.
- On someone's **birthday** (from Dates), and on his own, he wears a **party hat** (beside him while he sleeps). Some days have their own hat (a **Santa hat** from 25 December to 2 January, a **witch hat** on Halloween, **bunny ears** at Easter, a **heart** on 14 February), and on a few random days a month he puts one on just because.
- He comes in different ways: out of the ground, **dropping from the sky**, or (in the menu only) **thrown in from the side** by the menu's swing, which makes him **angry** for a moment. He comes in again when you're back after a while.
- Now and then things happen in his field: **bugs** crawl in and he fights them off with his **sword**, a **flower** comes up and he picks it, a **butterfly** flutters by, a **ball** rolls in and he kicks it, he does a **little dance**, and at dusk or night a **shooting star** goes over (the phone gives a little buzz wherever something lands, hits or clicks: a ball kicked, a bug struck, his battery dropping and clicking in, his hops, a bite, a dance beat). When a **notification** comes, his screen shows one. While a **medicine** is due (the red dot), he shows a pill on his screen and keeps at it until it's marked; it comes before anything else he'd do (not while he's asleep). After you've had a **recipe** open for 5 minutes, he has a meal when you next see him within the hour. Pip's page lists them all under Animations.
- While your phone is **plugged in**, his **charging station** stands in his field. If he isn't full, he hurries over, his hands pop in, and he plugs himself in, and his screen changes now and then. While you **watch** him there (here or on Pip Home), he gains a **segment every 8 seconds**; not watched, he uses energy as ever but can't run out. **Off**, the station sends out a **drone** that beams a segment into him; then he's up and goes to plug in. Once he's full he unplugs and goes about; full or asleep, he leaves it be. **Charging**, among his animations, shows it without a cable.
- **Adventures**: on his page, the **Adventures** button shows his adventure card; between 13:00 and 17:00 you can **register** him (a fanfare as he holds up his sword). Then every day from **12:00 to 18:00** he's out by himself: running, striking bugs, gaining **XP** (a level now and then, a **title** every 10 levels); sometimes a bug knocks him back and he has to go back for his sword. Look in between 14:00 and 16:00 and he takes a **break** at a tavern, sitting on its bench and eating at its table (if you just watched him set off, it waits till you've been away a few minutes). Out, he can't run out (he powers down after 18:00 if he must), and a tap still gives him a battery: it flies to him as he runs; off at 12:00, a battery before 17:00 sends him out. While he's out, the menu shows his **level**. Watch him and his XP goes up as he strikes bugs down ("+2 XP" over each), with bigger **beetles** and **spiders** now and then that take more strikes, and, if you're lucky, a **boss** once a day out (worth 50 XP). While he's out, his page opens on his adventure card; *Info* on it switches to his usual card and settings.
- **Ski trips**: on a Skiing holiday (Dates) his days are snowy, with a ski beanie and his gear in a snowbank; out, he **skis** down the slope instead (trees and flags coming up past him and away to the mountains, a fall now and then, a hot chocolate at a mountain hut for his break). Give the holiday **travel** hours and he rides a big **bus** there and back, in its window (asleep at night; nothing else meanwhile).
- **Quests**: on his page, under Adventures, the **Quests** button shows his quest card. His first quest is **Build a house**. Start it between 10:00 and 11:30 or 19:00 and 20:30, and from then on he works on it in those hours every day, even with the app closed. It takes about two months and can't be stopped. He walks out to the **forest**, the **mine** and the **beach** for wood, stone and sand, and builds the house piece by piece in his house area. He smelts glass in the fireplace, climbs a scaffold for the roof, wears a **rain hat** in the wet and works by a **lantern** after dark. While he works nothing else happens and he doesn't go to his station; a medicine reminder still shows on his screen. The **inventory** card (swipe from his card) shows what he's carrying, and a finished quest shows with a framed picture.
- Once the house is built it's **his home**: he's in it all day, out on his field only 11:30–12:00 and 18:00–19:00 (and on his adventures and ski trips). Inside he walks about, dances, looks out of the windows and warms himself by the fire. He sleeps there, his station comes up through the floor, and the weather stays outside. Bugs, flowers, the ball and the weather's games are for his field.
- His page in the Hub, **Pip**: his field, big, and a card; swipe it for his settings (rename him, play his animations and **adventures**, try a background or a **hat** on him, and under Background a **season**, the **weather**, one of his **days** or a **time of day** on his field: they go together, and are gone when you swipe back to his card or leave).

## Security
- A **master password** encrypts locked notes and can lock whole sections. Without it, locked data can't be read, so keep it safe.
- **Section locks** hide a whole section while the app is locked; under the Hub, each of its pages can be locked on its own.
- **Fingerprint** unlock can stand in for typing it. A locked Hub page or the to-do note asks as soon as you open it.
- The app locks again a little after you leave it.
- Forgot the password? **Forgot master password** resets it by deleting what it protects. To make sure it's you, type RESET and then **hold** Reset for 10 seconds; a plain tap turns it off for a day (or until you next unlock).
- Locked notes can't be deleted, and a backup can't be restored over them, without the password.

## Backup
- **Export** saves everything to one file; **Import** restores it. Locked notes stay encrypted inside.
- **Restore part of a backup** brings back only what you pick (say, one note), added to what's there.
- **Automatic backup** (on Backup): pick a folder once (say, one Google Drive syncs) and the app writes a backup there once a day when you open it, keeping the last 7.
- Backup shows when you last exported. A week later, a **red dot** on the menu and an evening notification remind you to make a new one (turn it off on Backup).

## Notifications
- The menu's **Notifications** shows the latest ones (tap **All** for today's and yesterday's), even after they're gone from the phone; tap one to go to it, **Clear** empties the list.
- Under it, each page's notifications: on or off, **pause** for a while, **private** (no names on the lock screen), a **sound** (the phone's, one of the app's 8-bit ones, or silent) with its **volume** (Loud, Medium, Quiet), and a **colour**.
- A notification stays in the phone's tray until you open its page (the Planner, Medicine, Dates, Backup) or swipe it away.
- If the phone blocks the app's notifications, this page says so at the top.

## Internet Access
- The app uses **no internet** unless you turn a service on in the menu's **Internet Access**. Nothing else leaves the phone: no accounts, no ads, no analytics.
- **Weather** (from Open-Meteo, free, no account): search your town or city and pick it from the matches (add more to switch between them). Pip's sky then shows the weather there (rain, snow, clouds, fog, storms), fetched every hour or so while the app is open. The page shows the weather now, when it was fetched, and a button to fetch it again. **Rain radar** (optional): forecasts can miss light rain, so for more accurate rain and snow right now, add your own free key from Tomorrow.io (the copy button copies where to sign up). It's checked once when you paste it; then the rain or snow now comes from it, every 15 minutes or so, and the weather line says so. The key stays on this phone (it isn't in backups) and is sent only to Tomorrow.io; if it stops working, the weather is Open-Meteo's alone and the page says why.
